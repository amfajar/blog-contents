> **Focus:** Your first intentional English output — private journaling and short writing. No pressure to be correct. The goal is to discover the gap between what you've absorbed and what you can currently produce, and to enjoy closing it.

---

> **Daily structure:** Anki Review (~20 min) → Session 1 Reading (40 min) → Session 2 Writing Output (20 min) → Session 3 Listening & Noticing (40 min) Input immersion during break time still counts as bonus — keep consuming freely.
