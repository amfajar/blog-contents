> **Focus:** Writing becomes public-facing — pieces you could theoretically share. Speaking practice begins in its simplest form: talking to yourself, recording yourself, noticing how your mouth and your mind connect.

---

> **Daily structure:** Anki Review (~20 min) → Session 1 Reading (40 min) → Session 2 Writing Output (20 min) → Session 3 Listening & Noticing (40 min)
