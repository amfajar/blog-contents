> **Focus:** Move from pure journaling into short structured pieces — observations, opinions, small essays. Start noticing the specific gaps between your input and output, and learn to work around them creatively rather than freezing.

---

> **Daily structure:** Anki Review (~20 min) → Session 1 Reading (40 min) → Session 2 Writing Output (20 min) → Session 3 Listening & Noticing (40 min)

---
