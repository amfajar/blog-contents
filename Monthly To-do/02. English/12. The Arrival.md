## 🌱 Year 2 Foundation

_You don't need a new roadmap to keep going. You need to keep going._

What Year 2 looks like:

- **Reading:** Move to harder literary fiction, non-fiction, essays — read whatever interests you
- **Listening:** No more "practising" listening — you're simply a person who consumes English content
- **Writing:** Keep publishing — find a consistent format that suits you (newsletter, blog, thread, essay)
- **Speaking:** Find regular conversation partners — weekly if possible, monthly at minimum
- **Anki:** Continue daily reviews — the deck will maintain itself; add new cards as you encounter them
- **The only rule:** Never stop — not forever, not for a month, not for a week if you can help it

_English is no longer something you're learning. It's something you are._
