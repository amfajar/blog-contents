> **Focus:** Push deeper into your second and third novels. Start noticing _how_ and _why_ people say things the way they do — not just _what_ they say.

---

> **Daily structure:** Anki Review (~20 min) → Session 1 Input (60 min) → Session 2 Listening & Noticing (40 min)
