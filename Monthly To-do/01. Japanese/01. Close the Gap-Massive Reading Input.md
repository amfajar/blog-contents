> **Focus:** Build the daily 8-hour habit. Establish Anki rhythm. Begin Light Novel reading with aggressive i+1 mining. Maintain listening gains. Attack the listening-reading gap from day one.
> 
> **Reading benchmark:** Current speed ~2,500 chars/hr. Each 90-min block = ~3,750 chars at current pace. Total reading per day = ~7,500 chars. Don't chase speed — consistency will move the number naturally.

---

### Day 1

#### Anki (60–90 min)

- [ ] Do all due reviews first — clear the queue before touching new cards
- [ ] Add 10 new cards (ease into the habit — don't burn out on Day 1)
- [ ] Confirm your card format: sentence on front, reading + meaning on back, audio if possible

#### Reading Block 1 (90 min)

- [ ] Open your LN epub — read forward without stopping for the first 10 minutes
- [ ] Target: reach ~3,000 chars this block (slightly under pace is fine today)
- [ ] On a second light pass, identify sentences with exactly one unknown element — mine 3–5 i+1 sentences into Anki
- [ ] Note your char position at end of block

#### Anime Block (90 min)

- [ ] Watch 2–3 episodes from your watchlist — no subtitles or JP subtitles only
- [ ] Don't look anything up during playback — just absorb
- [ ] Note 1–2 words or expressions that stood out (add to mining list if sentence is recoverable)

#### Reading Block 2 (90 min)

- [ ] Continue LN from your noted char position
- [ ] Target: another ~3,000 chars (total ~6,000 chars for the day — below full pace is fine on Day 1)
- [ ] Mine 2–3 more i+1 sentences if you find good ones
- [ ] If LN feels too heavy, switch to easier manga or graded reader for the last 30 min

#### Native Content (60–90 min)

- [ ] Watch one variety show, talk show, or YouTube video in natural Japanese
- [ ] No subtitles — casual listening immersion, not study
- [ ] Note any recurring expressions you want to mine later

#### Free Immersion (60 min)

- [ ] Anything enjoyable in Japanese — no pressure, no mining, just enjoy
