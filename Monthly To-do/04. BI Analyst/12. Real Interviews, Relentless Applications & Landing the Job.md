## 📝 Mindset Notes for the Long Job Search

**The timeline is unpredictable. Your daily output is not.**

- Most self-taught candidates get their first role in **3–9 months** of active searching after their portfolio is ready
- Silence from companies is normal — it's not a reflection of your quality
- 1 callback from 20 applications is a reasonable early-stage rate — improve the ratio by iterating your materials
- The weekly template exists so that whether week 4 or week 24, you're still sharp and still moving
- Skills don't degrade if you maintain them — but they will if you don't
- Getting rejected is data. Analyze it like a BI Analyst would.
- The offer is coming — you just don't know which application it will come from
